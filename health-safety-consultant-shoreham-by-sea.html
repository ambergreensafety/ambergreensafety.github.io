Local Health & Safety Support in Shoreham-by-Sea

Ambergreen Safety provides straightforward Health & Safety advice and support for organisations working in Shoreham-by-Sea and nearby areas. We are based in Worthing and support clients across West Sussex and appropriate parts of East Sussex.

We are NEBOSH-qualified consultants with over 30 years of experience. Our service is particularly suitable for small and medium-sized businesses, contractors and construction companies that need competent professional support without employing a full-time Health & Safety manager.

Shoreham-by-Sea combines port, marine and industrial activity with construction, logistics, offices, retail and a growing population of smaller enterprises. We provide proportionate support suited to both higher-risk operational work and lower-risk commercial workplaces.

Support can be provided for a one-off requirement, a particular project or as continuing access to professional advice.

Practical Support for Local Employers

Health & Safety arrangements should protect people and help the business comply with its responsibilities without creating avoidable bureaucracy. We first seek to understand your activities, workforce and significant hazards. We can then review what is already in place, explain any important gaps and help establish proportionate controls.

Documents are prepared to reflect the actual work being undertaken. This makes them easier for managers, employees and contractors to understand and use.

Construction Health & Safety in Shoreham-by-Sea

Ambergreen Safety supports contractors, subcontractors, property businesses and clients involved in construction, refurbishment and maintenance work. Assistance can include RAMS, Construction Phase Plans, CDM advice, site inspections, contractor arrangements, tender information and continuing Competent Person support.

The service is scaled to the project and concentrates on the significant risks, required controls and clear evidence that appropriate arrangements are in place.

Competent Person Services

Employers must have access to competent Health & Safety assistance. For an organisation without sufficient in-house expertise, Ambergreen Safety can provide external Competent Person support. This may include telephone and email advice, reviews of policies and assessments, help responding to client requirements and further consultancy when required.

Risk Assessments, RAMS and Documentation

We prepare and review risk assessments, method statements, Health & Safety policies, COSHH assessments, workplace procedures and other essential documentation. The aim is to identify the significant hazards, set out sensible precautions and give your business documentation it can realistically maintain.

Audits, Inspections and SSIP Support

An audit or inspection can identify weaknesses before they lead to injury, enforcement action, failed contractor assessments or business disruption. We provide clear findings and practical priorities. We can also help contractors demonstrate their arrangements for CHAS, SafeContractor, Constructionline and other SSIP or pre-qualification requirements.
