Health & Safety Competent Person Service in Worthing & Sussex

Practical ongoing Health & Safety advice for businesses and contractors who need access to a competent person without employing a full-time Health & Safety adviser.

What is a Health & Safety Competent Person?

Businesses need access to competent Health & Safety assistance appropriate to the nature and risks of their activities. Ambergreen Safety provides an external Competent Person service for organisations that require professional Health & Safety advice but do not need a full-time in-house adviser.

We provide practical competent safety advice to help businesses understand their responsibilities, maintain appropriate documentation and manage workplace risks effectively.

External Competent Person support

Appointing Ambergreen Safety as your external Competent Person gives your business access to ongoing professional Health & Safety support when you need it. The level of assistance can be tailored to suit your organisation, workforce and activities.

What our Competent Person service includes

  • Ongoing Health & Safety advice and guidance
  • Health & Safety Policy reviews
  • Risk assessment and RAMS reviews
  • Health & Safety procedures and documentation
  • Advice on workplace Health & Safety requirements
  • Accident, incident and near-miss advice
  • Workplace inspections and Health & Safety audits
  • Support with SSIP accreditation and pre-qualification
  • Construction Health & Safety and CDM advice
  • Practical recommendations and follow-up support

Who is the service suitable for?

Our Competent Person service is particularly suitable for small and medium-sized businesses, contractors and organisations that need reliable Health & Safety support without the cost and commitment of employing a full-time Health & Safety manager.

The service can also support businesses that need to demonstrate that they have access to competent Health & Safety advice for clients, insurers, accreditation schemes or contractor assessments.

Competent Person support in Worthing, Sussex and the South East

Ambergreen Safety provides Competent Person support for businesses in Worthing, West Sussex, Brighton & Hove, Shoreham, Littlehampton, Horsham, Crawley and surrounding Sussex areas.

Support can be provided on an ongoing basis or as required, depending on the needs of your business.

Practical and proportionate Health & Safety advice

Our approach is to provide the level of Health & Safety support appropriate to your business, activities and risks. The objective is effective Health & Safety management and sensible compliance rather than unnecessary paperwork.
