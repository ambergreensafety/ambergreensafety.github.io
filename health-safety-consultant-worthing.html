Competent Person Health & Safety Support

Employers are required to have access to competent Health & Safety assistance.

Ambergreen Safety can provide ongoing Competent Person support, giving your business access to professional Health & Safety advice when it is needed.

Risk Assessments and RAMS

Risk assessments should identify the significant hazards associated with your activities and set out sensible precautions to control those risks.

We can prepare, review or improve workplace and activity risk assessments and provide clear Risk Assessments and Method Statements for contractors and construction projects.

Documents are written to reflect the actual work being undertaken rather than relying on unnecessary generic paperwork.

Health & Safety Audits and Inspections

A Health & Safety audit or workplace inspection can help identify weaknesses before they result in accidents, enforcement action or disruption to your business.

We provide practical findings and recommendations, concentrating on significant issues and reasonable actions.

Not every small business needs to employ a Health & Safety professional. However, employers must ensure that they have access to competent assistance to help them meet their Health & Safety responsibilities. External professional support can be a cost-effective way of achieving this.

Yes. Ambergreen Safety provides a Competent Person service for businesses that require continuing access to professional Health & Safety advice without employing their own full-time Health & Safety manager.
